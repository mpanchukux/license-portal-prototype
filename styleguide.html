Design system

Every component on these pages is rendered by the product’s own builders, from the same styles.css the portal loads. Nothing here is a screenshot or a copy: change a rule and this page changes with it, and a combination that stops rendering stops rendering here too.

The rules and the axes of components usage and behaviour are in COMPONENTS.md. These pages show what a component looks like and which states it has; that file says what it is for and what it may not do.

How to read a page

Each specimen is labelled with the class you write to get it. Where a component has states, they are a matrix: forms down, states across, a live control in every cell. Hover, focus and press are the browser’s, so those columns are labelled rather than drawn — read them with a pointer and the Tab key.

A cell showing — means the component has no such state, and that is a fact about the product rather than a gap in the page.

What is not here

Page specifications, copy rules and the reasoning behind a decision are not on these pages. Surfaces live in NOTES.md, the scales in SCALES.md, contrast measurements in CONTRAST.md. A tab here that carries only prose is a document standing in a rail of components — two were removed for that reason.

Spacing & layout

The page container and the one control height every toolbar aligns to.

Radii

Eight values: 0, 4, 8, 12 and 24 are sizes; pill and circle are shapes. Interactive controls take 24, small surfaces take 8 — and 24 renders as a pill on every control in the product, which is measured and deliberately kept.

What each is for. sharp a surface meeting another flush · tight focus rings and small marks · control menus, popovers, cards, modal headers — small surfaces, not inputs · surface cards, menus, dialogs · feature the plan card · interactive every interactive control: button, field, search, stepper, the Instances switcher · pill and circle are shapes, not sizes.
